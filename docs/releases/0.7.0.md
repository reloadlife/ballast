# Ballast 0.7.0 — Chart Under Glass

Ballast has a new look and knows much more about what it is cleaning. Every screen now reads your disk like a nautical chart: free space is open water, the weight on the disk is drawn in depth bands of blue, and the tools you act on float above it on Liquid Glass.

- **A new Overview:** one depth bar shows what fills the disk, from applications and your files to caches, build files and System Data, with free space as the headline figure. "Can be cleaned safely" sits right under it, followed by your largest folders and files and how the disk has changed over time.
- **The Ballast dock:** anything you add to the Cleanup List gathers at the bottom of the window: how many items, how much space, and how many need you. Review opens the list when you're ready.
- **A rebuilt Cleanup List:** items are grouped by kind, and anything that needs a decision is listed first with exactly what it waits on: quit an app, or confirm you've reviewed it. Cleanups show each item ticking off and can be stopped part way. Afterwards you see what came back, with Put Back and Cleanup History one click away.
- **A cleanup knowledge base:** Ballast knows what over a hundred kinds of common folders are for and checks that before its general rules. Folders that sign you out of a site, photo and music libraries, project files and anything personal in Documents, Desktop, Music, Movies or Pictures are now marked for review instead of quick removal. **What is this?** explains any folder in plain words.
- **Smarter Suggestions:** per-app caches, large music, video and photo folders (50 MB and up) and build folders with their age.
- **Homebrew cleanup that removes only what it shows:** preview and remove leftover dependencies and old versions. Uninstalls and the Homebrew suggestion no longer take unlisted dependencies with them.
- **Explorer, System Data and Health on the chart:** a clearer treemap, every System Data load finishes with an explanation of each part, and Health results are coded by shape as well as color.
- **Steadier and faster:** verdicts load in chunks so long lists stay responsive, admin scans can be cancelled, and the Cleanup List no longer freezes or crashes the window when it opens.

Ad-hoc signed (not notarized): on first launch, open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Ballast.app`.
